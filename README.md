## 🔬 Core Research Focus

Modern Large Language Models suffer from a fundamental category mistake: **equating sequential surface tokens with thinking**. Human cognition is continuous, high-dimensional, and dynamical—language is merely peripheral I/O.

My research focuses on replacing token-by-token `<think>` chains with:
1. **Rank-4 Non-Token Latent Thought Matrices ($\mathbf{M} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times S \times 4 \times D}$)**: Simultaneously maintaining unperturbed factual anchors, wild lateral exploration, epistemic veracity scoring, and Banach structural deduction in strictly orthogonal subspaces ($\mathbf{M}_k \perp \mathbf{M}_0$).
2. **Recurrent Toroidal Central Cognitive Routers ("Otak Berputar")**: Circulating cognitive updates across cyclic ring-attention topology ($C = 2 \dots 12$ micro-cycles) with homeostatic relative norm bounds ($\|\Delta \mathbf{h}_\perp\|_2 / \|\mathbf{M}_0\|_2 \le 0.0150$).
3. **Neuromorphic Event-Driven Spiking Gates**: Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neurons with FastSigmoid surrogate gradients and homeostatic threshold adaptation to modulate cognitive activation with sub-microsecond latency.
4. **Rigid Numeric & AST Symbolic Registers**: Deterministic 64-dimensional Euclidean subspaces for exact ALU computation, calendar cyclic groups ($\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}$), and static zero-RCE abstract syntax tree verification.
